About the Institute

Quality technical education, built for the job market

Emurua Dikirr Technical Training Institute is a public TVET institution in Narok County, established in September 2019 under the Kenyan government's Vision 2030 initiative. We deliver competency-based training designed to prepare trainees for employment and entrepreneurship.

  • Competency-Based Education & Training (CBET) curriculum
  • Modern labs, workshops and a Jitume digital skills lab
  • Pathways from Level 3 through Level 6 qualifications
  • Government funding support via the Higher Education Fund (HEF)

"We don't just teach trades — we prepare graduates who are globally competitive and ready to build."

— The EDTTI promise
