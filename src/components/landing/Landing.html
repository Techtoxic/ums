About EDTTI

Emurua Dikirr Technical Training Institute is a public TVET institution located in Narok County, established in September 2019 under the Kenyan government's Vision 2030 initiative. We provide quality technical and vocational education designed to prepare our trainees for the global job market and entrepreneurship.
