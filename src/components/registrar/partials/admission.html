Admission Rules

  • • Admission numbers are globally unique, starting at 2500.
  • • Required fields: name, ID/birth-certificate number, phone, course, KCSE grade, intake, admission type.
  • • Grade rules — Level 4 accepts E or D-; Level 5 accepts D or D+; Level 6 accepts C- through B+; Level 3 accepts KCPE.
  • • Each course's grade dropdown filters automatically to the grades allowed for its level.
