Privacy Policy

Emurua Dikirr Technical Training Institute · Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Emurua Dikirr Technical Training Institute ("EDTTI", "we", "us") collects, uses, protects and shares personal information through the EDTTI University Management System (the "System"). By using the System you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to administer training, examinations, finance and student welfare, including:

  • Identity & contact details — name, national ID/birth-certificate number, admission number, staff ID, phone number, email and postal address.
  • Academic records — course, department, module, unit registrations, assessments, attendance and graduation/attachment applications.
  • Financial records — fee balances, payments and payslip information (for staff).
  • Account & security data — login credentials (passwords are stored only as salted hashes), session cookies and basic activity logs used to keep the System secure.
  • Next-of-kin information provided during admission.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To register and manage students, trainers and staff and deliver training services.
  • To process admissions, unit registrations, assessments, attachments and graduation.
  • To manage fees, payments, receipts and staff payslips.
  • To communicate important notices (e.g. payslips, application status, account security).
  • To secure the System, prevent abuse and comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where necessary:

  • With authorised EDTTI staff (administration, registry, finance, deans, trainers, HODs) on a need-to-know basis.
  • With Government bodies and regulators where required by law (e.g. TVETA, KUCCPS, examination bodies).
  • With service providers that help us operate the System (e.g. email delivery), under confidentiality obligations.

4. Cookies

The System uses strictly necessary cookies only: a secure, httpOnly session cookie to keep you signed in and a CSRF-protection cookie. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Disabling these cookies will prevent you from logging in.

5. Data Security

We protect your information using industry-standard measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, role-based access control, session expiry and server-side audit logging. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we work continually to safeguard your data.

6. Data Retention

We retain academic and financial records for as long as required for institutional, legal and regulatory purposes. Account and security logs are kept only as long as needed to operate and protect the System.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, or correction of, the personal data we hold about you, subject to verification and legal limits. Students should contact the Registry; staff should contact Administration.

8. Children & Students

Where students are below the age of majority, account information is managed by the institution and accessible to authorised guardians/next-of-kin where appropriate.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the System after changes constitutes acceptance.
